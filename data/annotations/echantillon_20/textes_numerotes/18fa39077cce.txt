00296 | 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS
00297 | 5.1 Anaphylactic Reactions
00298 | Serious and occasionally fatal hypersensitivity (anaphylactic) reactions have been
00299 | reported in patients on β-lactam antibacterials, including cefuroxime axetil [see Adverse
00300 | Reactions (6.2)] . These reactions are more likely to occur in individuals with a history of
00301 | β-lactam hypersensitivity and/or a history of sensitivity to multiple allergens. There have
00302 | been reports of individuals with a history of penicillin hypersensitivity who have
00303 | experienced severe reactions when treated with cephalosporins. Cefuroxime axetil is
00304 | contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to cefuroxime axetil or other β-
00305 | b
00306 | 
00307 | lactam antibacterial drugs [see Contraindications (4)] . Before initiating therapy with
00308 | cefuroxime axetil, inquire about previous hypersensitivity reactions to penicillins,
00309 | cephalosporins, or other allergens. If an allergic reaction occurs, discontinue cefuroxime
00310 | axetil and institute appropriate therapy.
00311 | 5.2 Clostridioides difficile-Associated Diarrhea
00312 | Clostridioidesdifficile- associated diarrhea (CDAD) has been reported with use of nearly all
00313 | antibacterial agents, including cefuroxime axetil, and may range in severity from mild
00314 | diarrhea to fatal colitis. Treatment with antibacterial agents alters the normal flora of the
00315 | colon leading to overgrowth of C. difficile. 
00316 | C. difficile produces toxins A and B which contribute to the development of CDAD.
00317 | Hypertoxin-producing strains of C. difficilecause increased morbidity and mortality, as
00318 | these infections can be refractory to antimicrobial therapy and may require colectomy.
00319 | CDAD must be considered in all patients who present with diarrhea following antibiotic
00320 | use. Careful medical history is necessary since CDAD has been reported to occur over 2
00321 | months after the administration of antibacterial agents. 
00322 | If CDAD is suspected or confirmed, ongoing antibiotic use not directed against C.
00323 | difficilemay need to be discontinued. Appropriate fluid and electrolyte management,
00324 | protein supplementation, antibiotic treatment of C. difficile, and surgical evaluation
00325 | should be instituted as clinically indicated.
00326 | 5.3 Potential for Microbial Overgrowth
00327 | The possibility of superinfections with fungal or bacterial pathogens should be
00328 | considered during therapy.
00329 | 5.4 Development of Drug-Resistant Bacteria
00330 | Prescribing cefuroxime axetil either in the absence of a proven or strongly suspected
00331 | bacterial infection or a prophylactic indication is unlikely to provide benefit to the patient
00332 | and increases the risk of the development of drug-resistant bacteria.
00333 | 5.6 Interference with Glucose Tests
00334 | A false-positive result for glucose in the urine may occur with copper reduction tests,
00335 | and a false-negative result for blood/plasma glucose may occur with ferricyanide tests in
00336 | subjects receiving cefuroxime axetil [see Drug Interactions (7.3)] .

00455 | 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS
00456 | 7.1 Drugs that Reduce Gastric Acidity
00457 | Drugs that reduce gastric acidity may result in a lower bioavailability of cefuroxime axetil
00458 | compared with administration in the fasting state. Administration of drugs that reduce
00459 | gastric acidity may negate the food effect of increased absorption of cefuroxime axetil
00460 | when administered in the postprandial state. Administer cefuroxime axetil at least 1 hour
00461 | before or 2 hours after administration of short-acting antacids. Histamine-2 (H )
00462 | antagonists and proton pump inhibitors should be avoided.
00463 | 7.2 Probenecid
00464 | Concomitant administration of probenecid with cefuroxime axetil tablets increases
00465 | serum concentrations of cefuroxime [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
00466 | Coadministration of probenecid with cefuroxime axetil is not recommended.
00467 | 7.3 Drug/Laboratory Test Interactions
00468 | A false-positive reaction for glucose in the urine may occur with copper reduction tests
00469 | (e.g., Benedict's or Fehling's solution), but not with enzyme-based tests for glycosuria.
00470 | As a false-negative result may occur in the ferricyanide test, it is recommended that
00471 | either the glucose oxidase or hexokinase method be used to determine blood/plasma
00472 | glucose levels in patients receiving cefuroxime axetil. The presence of cefuroxime does
00473 | not interfere with the assay of serum and urine creatinine by the alkaline picrate method.
00474 | 8 USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS
00475 | 8.1 Pregnancy
00476 | Risk Summary
00477 | Available data from published epidemiologic studies, case series, and case reports over
00478 | several decades with cephalosporin use, including cefuroxime axetil, in pregnant women
00479 | have not established drug-associated risks of major birth defects, miscarriage, or
00480 | adverse maternal or fetal outcomes (see Data). 
00481 | In studies in pregnant mice and rats administered oral cefuroxime axetil during
00482 | organogenesis at 14 and 9 times the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD)
00483 | based on body surface area, respectively, there were no adverse developmental
00484 | outcomes (see Data). 
00485 | 2
00486 | 
00487 | The estimated background risk of major birth defects and miscarriage for the indicated
00488 | populations are unknown. All pregnancies have a background risk of birth defects, loss,
00489 | or other adverse outcomes. In the U.S. general population, the estimated background
00490 | risk of major birth defects and miscarriage in clinically recognized pregnancies is 2% to
00491 | 4% and 15% to 20%, respectively. 
00492 | Clinical Considerations 
00493 | Disease-Associated Maternal and/or Embryo/Fetal Risk: Maternal gonorrhea may be
00494 | associated with preterm birth, low neonatal birth weight, chorioamnionitis, intrauterine
00495 | growth restriction, small for gestational age, and premature rupture of membranes.
00496 | Perinatal transmission of gonorrhea to the offspring can result in infant blindness, joint
00497 | infections, and bloodstream infections. 
00498 | Data 
00499 | Human Data: While available studies cannot definitively establish the absence of risk,

00512 | Risk Summary

00535 | Of the total number of subjects who received cefuroxime axetil in 20 clinical trials, 375

00539 | between the elderly and younger adult patients, but greater sensitivity of some older
